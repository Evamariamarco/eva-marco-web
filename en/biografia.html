Biography

A Spanish lyric soprano with “a voice of warm colour and generous top notes” (Mundoclasico), Eva Marco has brought to life Mimì, Desdemona, Countess Almaviva, Donna Elvira, Norma and Tosca on stages in Spain, Italy, Greece, the United Kingdom, the United States and France.

She trained in Spain and in Milan with soprano Nino Machaidze, and in 2022 completed the Master’s in Operatic Performance at the Bel Canto Academy in Trento with Massimo Lambertini.

She made her debut in 2020 as Idamante (Idomeneo, Mozart) on an international tour of Greece. Her first Mimì (La Bohème) followed in 2022 with the Las Palmas Philharmonic Orchestra and, a year later, the symphonic tour “Puccini’s Heroines”. In 2024 she sang Desdemona (Otello, Verdi) in Spanish theatres and Countess Almaviva (Le nozze di Figaro) at the Romanian National Opera in Cluj.

In the oratorio and concert repertoire she has sung Bach’s St John Passion (2025) and Mozart’s Mass in C minor (2026) at the Auditorio Nacional in Madrid. In 2026 she shared the stage with Plácido Domingo at a lyric gala in Santander and closed the Gurjaani International Arts Festival in Georgia.

On stage she stars in “María Callas: Sfogato”, a theatrical tribute to the great diva that critics rated 4.5 out of 5. Her next challenge is Aida.
